# Australia — Study Abroad & Student Visa Specification (Bangladeshi Students)

**Target Jurisdiction:** Commonwealth of Australia  
**Visa Subclass:** Student visa (Subclass 500)  
**Primary Immigration Authority:** Department of Home Affairs (DHA)  
**Official Portal:** [https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500)  
**Dhaka High Commission:** Australian High Commission, 184 Gulshan Avenue, Dhaka  

---

## 1. Key Academic Requirements for Bangladeshi Students

- **Genuine Student (GS) Criterion:**
  - Replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) requirement.
  - Requires targeted statements proving academic justification, career progression in Bangladesh, and thorough research of course modules.
- **Undergraduate:**
  - HSC GPA: 4.20/5.00+ for direct bachelor entry (or Foundation / Diploma pathway for GPA 3.50+).
- **Postgraduate:**
  - 4-year recognized Bachelor's degree (CGPA 2.80+ for mid-tier; 3.20+ for Group of Eight universities).
- **Language Requirements (Updated 2024/2026):**
  - Minimum IELTS Academic score for student visa: **6.0 overall** (up from 5.5 previously).
  - Most universities require IELTS 6.5 (minimum 6.0 each band) for master's programs.

---

## 2. Financial Capacity Requirements

- **Living Costs (12 Months):** **AUD 29,710**
- **Tuition Fee:** 1 full year tuition as specified on Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE).
- **Travel Costs:** **AUD 2,000** return airfare.
- **Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC):** Approx. **AUD 600 - 800/year** mandatory for duration of visa.
- **Total Calculation Formula:**  
  `Total Funds = 1st Year Tuition + AUD 29,710 + AUD 2,000 + OSHC`
- **Holding Period & Fund Quality:**
  - Funds held for at least 3 consecutive months in approved scheduled banks in Bangladesh, or an approved educational loan from an authorized financial institution.

---

## 3. Post-Study Work (Subclass 485 Temporary Graduate Visa)

- **Duration:**
  - Bachelor's degree: **2 years**
  - Master's by Coursework / Research: **2 years**
  - PhD / Doctoral degree: **3 years**
- **Regional Study Extension:**
  - Additional 1 to 2 years post-study work permit available for studying and living in designated regional areas (e.g. Adelaide, Perth, Wollongong, Newcastle).
