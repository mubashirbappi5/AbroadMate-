# Germany — Study Abroad & Student Visa Specification (Bangladeshi Students)

**Target Jurisdiction:** Federal Republic of Germany  
**Visa Category:** National Visa for Study (Type D / Visum zu Studienzwecken)  
**Primary Immigration Authority:** Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt) / BAMF  
**Official Portal:** [https://www.make-it-in-germany.com](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com)  
**Dhaka Embassy:** German Embassy Dhaka, Madani Avenue, Baridhara, Dhaka 1212  

---

## 1. Key Academic Requirements for Bangladeshi Students

- **Undergraduate (Bachelor's):**
  - Direct admission after HSC is generally **NOT possible** due to difference in 12-year vs. 13-year German Abitur schooling system.
  - Pathways:
    1. Complete 1 year of successful university education at an H+ accredited Bangladeshi university (e.g. DU, BUET, NSU, BRAC).
    2. Pass the **Feststellungsprüfung (FSP)** via a **Studienkolleg** foundation course in Germany.
  - A-Levels: Minimum 3 A-Levels with specified math/language subjects recognized by the *anabin* database.
- **Postgraduate (Master's):**
  - 4-year Bachelor's degree from an H+ recognized university in Bangladesh.
  - Minimum ECTS credit alignment and CGPA (typically minimum 3.00/4.00; competitive programs demand 3.30+).
- **Language Requirements:**
  - English-taught courses: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 per component).
  - German-taught courses: TestDaF (minimum 4x4) or Goethe-Zertifikat C1.

---

## 2. Tuition Fee Structure in Germany

- **Public Universities:**
  - Tuition-free in 15 of 16 German federal states (Baden-Württemberg charges €1,500/semester for non-EU students; Technical University of Munich TUM introduced specific non-EU fees for some programs).
  - Mandatory **Semesterbeitrag** (Semester Contribution): €250 – €380 per semester, which includes the regional Semesterticket for public transit.
- **Private Universities:**
  - Tuition ranges from €8,000 to €20,000+ per year.

---

## 3. Financial Proof & Blocked Account (Sperrkonto)

- **Mandatory Blocked Account Requirement:**
  - For the 2025/2026 academic year, students must deposit **€11,904** (approx. **€992 per month** for 12 months).
  - Approved Blocked Account Providers: **Expatrio, Coracle, Fintiba, Deutsche Bank**.
- **Alternative:**
  - Official Declaration of Commitment (*Verpflichtungserklärung*) from a permanent resident or citizen in Germany with verified financial solvency.

---

## 4. Post-Study Work & Settlement

- **Job-Seeking Residence Permit (§ 20 Abs. 3 Nr. 1 AufenthG):**
  - Upon graduating from a recognized German university, graduates are granted an **18-month Jobseeker Residence Permit**.
  - During these 18 months, graduates can work full-time in any job while seeking career-aligned employment.
- **EU Blue Card & Permanent Residency (Niederlassungserlaubnis):**
  - German university graduates can qualify for German Permanent Residency (PR) after working just **21 to 24 months** on a skilled residence permit while contributing to pension insurance and proving B1 German proficiency.
