# United States — Study Abroad & Student Visa Specification (Bangladeshi Students)

**Target Jurisdiction:** United States of America  
**Visa Category:** F-1 Academic Student Visa  
**Primary Authorities:** U.S. Department of State (Consular Affairs) & SEVP / DHS  
**Official Portals:** [https://travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov) & [https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov)  
**Dhaka Embassy:** Embassy of the United States of America, Madani Avenue, Baridhara, Dhaka  

---

## 1. Key Academic Requirements for Bangladeshi Students

- **SEVP-Certified Institution & Form I-20:**
  - Student must be accepted by a Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP)-certified school.
  - Institution issues **Form I-20 (Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status)** with unique SEVIS ID.
- **Undergraduate:**
  - HSC GPA 4.00+ or O/A Levels.
  - SAT / ACT: Optional in many institutions, but submitting competitive scores (1300+ SAT) significantly improves scholarship prospects.
- **Graduate (Master's / PhD):**
  - 4-year Bachelor's degree (CGPA 3.00/4.00+; competitive STEM departments prefer 3.40+).
  - GRE / GMAT: Waived by many universities post-pandemic; strongly recommended for funded graduate assistantships (TA/RA).
- **English Proficiency:**
  - IELTS Academic: 6.5 - 7.0 overall.
  - TOEFL iBT: 80 - 100 overall.
  - Duolingo English Test (DET): 110 - 130 accepted by many institutions.

---

## 2. Financial Requirements & Form I-20 Funding

- **Total First-Year Estimated Cost:**
  - Listed explicitly on Form I-20 Section 7 (Tuition + Living Expenses + Books/Insurance).
  - Typically ranges between **$25,000 to $65,000 USD** depending on institution (Public vs. Private).
- **Financial Proof for F-1 Visa Interview:**
  - Liquid funds (Savings, Fixed Deposits) covering at least the 1st full academic year amount.
  - Demonstrable income source of sponsor (Father/Mother/Self) proving capability to fund subsequent years (Income tax returns, salary certificates, business trade licenses, agricultural income proofs).
- **Mandatory Government Fees:**
  - **SEVIS I-901 Fee:** $350 (Paid directly on fmjfee.com).
  - **MRV Visa Application Fee (DS-160):** $185 (Paid to designated bank in Dhaka prior to interview scheduling).

---

## 3. In-Person Visa Interview (Dhaka Embassy)

Under INA Section 214(b), every applicant is presumed to be an intending immigrant until they establish:
1. They are a bona fide student with a credible academic plan.
2. They have sufficient funds to study without engaging in unauthorized employment.
3. They have strong socio-economic ties to Bangladesh ensuring departure upon completion of study.

---

## 4. Post-Study Work & Optional Practical Training (OPT)

- **Standard Post-Completion OPT:** **12 months** of temporary employment in student's field of study.
- **STEM OPT Extension:**
  - Graduates with qualifying STEM degrees (Science, Technology, Engineering, Math) receive an additional **24-month extension** (Total **36 months** / 3 years of work authorization in the USA).
- **H-1B Cap-Gap & Transition:**
  - Eligible for employer sponsorship for H-1B specialty occupation visa while on OPT.
