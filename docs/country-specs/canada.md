# Canada — Study Abroad & Student Visa Specification (Bangladeshi Students)

**Target Jurisdiction:** Canada (All Provinces)  
**Visa / Permit Subclass:** Study Permit (with Temporary Resident Visa - TRV)  
**Primary Immigration Authority:** Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)  
**Official Portal:** [https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada.html)  
**Dhaka High Commission:** High Commission of Canada in Bangladesh, Diplomatic Enclave, Dhaka  

---

## 1. Key Academic Requirements for Bangladeshi Students

- **Designated Learning Institution (DLI) & PAL:**
  - Acceptance from an approved DLI university or college.
  - Mandatory **Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL)** issued by the provincial government (e.g. Ontario, British Columbia) confirming the student falls under the national student cap allocation.
- **Undergraduate (Bachelor's):**
  - Minimum HSC GPA: 4.00/5.00+ (Science/Commerce/Humanities).
  - A-Levels: Minimum 3 subjects with competitive grades.
- **Postgraduate (Master's):**
  - 4-year Bachelor's degree with minimum 65-75% marks (approx. CGPA 3.00 - 3.20+ on a 4.00 scale).
- **Language Requirements:**
  - IELTS Academic: Minimum 6.0 overall for undergrad; minimum 6.5 overall (minimum 6.0 each band) for postgrad.
  - PTE Academic (Score 60+) and TOEFL iBT (Score 85+) also widely accepted.

---

## 2. Financial Proof & Living Expenses

- **Cost of Living Benchmark (Updated IRCC 2024/2026):**
  - Minimum living requirement: **CAD 20,635** for 1 year (excluding 1st year tuition).
- **Proof of Funds:**
  - Proof of full payment of 1st year tuition fee to the DLI.
  - 4 to 6 months continuous bank history showing verified and legitimate source of funds (salary slips, tax returns/TIN, land valuation, business documents).
- **Sponsorship:**
  - Primary sponsors: Parents (Father/Mother). Third-party sponsorship faces severe scrutiny under IRCC section 216(1).

---

## 3. Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)

- **Eligibility:**
  - Programs of 8 months to 2 years: PGWP valid for the length of study.
  - Master's programs (even 1-year or 16-month duration): Eligible for a **3-year PGWP**.
- **Recent Reforms:**
  - College diplomas must align with national in-demand occupational shortages (e.g. Healthcare, STEM, Skilled Trades). University bachelor's and master's degrees retain broad eligibility.
