# United Kingdom — Study Abroad & Student Visa Specification (Bangladeshi Students)

**Target Jurisdiction:** England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland  
**Visa Subclass:** Student Visa (Student Route)  
**Primary Immigration Authority:** UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) / Home Office  
**Official Portal:** [https://www.gov.uk/student-visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)  
**Dhaka High Commission:** British High Commission, United Nations Road, Baridhara, Dhaka  

---

## 1. Key Academic Requirements for Bangladeshi Students

- **Undergraduate (Bachelor's):**
  - Standard Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) GPA of 4.50+ may be considered by some universities; however, many Russell Group universities require an **International Foundation Programme (IFP)** or **International Year One (IY1)**.
  - A-Levels: Minimum 3 A-Level subjects with grades typically BBB to A*AA depending on course competitiveness.
  - O-Levels / IGCSE: Minimum 5 passes with grades A*-C.
- **Postgraduate (Master's):**
  - 4-year Bachelor's degree from a recognized Bangladeshi university (UGC approved).
  - Minimum CGPA required: 3.00/4.00 (Equivalent to UK 2:2) or 3.25/4.00+ (Equivalent to UK 2:1).
- **English Proficiency:**
  - Bachelor's: IELTS Academic 6.0 overall (no band less than 5.5).
  - Master's: IELTS Academic 6.5 overall (no band less than 6.0).
  - PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, and Oxford ELLT are also accepted by specific universities.

## 2. Financial Solvency & Proof of Funds (UK 28-Day Rule)

The UKVI strictly enforces the **28-Day Consecutive Holding Rule**:
1. **Tuition Fee:** 1st year tuition fee minus any deposit paid to the university.
2. **Living Expenses:**
   - **Inside London:** £1,334 per month for up to 9 months = **£12,006**.
   - **Outside London:** £1,023 per month for up to 9 months = **£9,207**.
3. **Holding Period:**
   - The entire required amount (Remaining Tuition + 9 Months Living Expenses) must be held in a designated bank account for a continuous period of at least **28 consecutive days**.
   - The end date of the 28-day period must be within **31 days** of the date you submit your online visa application.
4. **Sponsorship Rules:**
   - Funds must be held in the student's personal account or their parent's (father/mother) account.
   - If using parents' account: Original birth certificate, parent consent letter, and parent affidavit must be provided.
   - Friends, uncles, cousins, or third-party company accounts are strictly inadmissible.

---

## 3. Visa Application Fees & Surcharges (as of 2025/2026)

- **Student Visa Application Fee:** £490
- **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS):** £776 per year of study (allows access to the National Health Service - NHS).
- **VFS Biometrics & TB Screening:**
  - Tuberculosis (TB) test certificate from an approved IOM clinic in Dhaka or Sylhet is mandatory before visa lodgement.
  - VFS Global Dhaka / Sylhet appointment for biometric enrollment.

---

## 4. Post-Study Work (PSW) — Graduate Route Visa

- **Duration:**
  - Bachelor's & Master's graduates: **2 years** of unsponsored full-time work rights.
  - Doctoral / PhD graduates: **3 years** of unsponsored full-time work rights.
- **Switching Route:**
  - Graduates can switch from the Graduate Route into the Skilled Worker Visa if sponsored by an eligible UK employer.
